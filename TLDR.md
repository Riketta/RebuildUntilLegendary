# Rebuild Until LEGENDARY

Keep rebuilding a building until it reaches the quality you want - and stop by itself.

## The rebuild loop

- Toggle "Rebuild until..." on a building - an already placed blueprint or a partially built frame works too - pick the target quality and who may build it: anyone or one specific pawn.
- Every finished result below the target gets a normal vanilla deconstruct order (usual material refund), and once it is down a fresh blueprint is placed on the same spot with the same material, style, rotation and storage settings. As soon as the target quality or better is rolled, the loop switches itself off with a success message.
- With a specific pawn chosen, only that pawn does the construction - every attempt is a full vanilla construction job, so the builder earns experience each time, and as skill rises the rolls converge on masterwork and legendary by themselves.
- Training mode cancels building frames at 99% work instead of finishing them: 100% material refund, nearly all experience kept - each cycle is almost free, ideal for leveling a rookie on cheap furniture.
- The loop never fights the player: canceling the blueprint, replacing the building or moving it away stops the loop cleanly, and a running rebuild survives saving and loading.
